01Listings & search

Listings, sorting & filters

Users browse rent and sale listings, sort them by nearest, price or most recent, and narrow results with filters for city, district, transaction type and property type. Recent searches are kept, filters can be saved, and ads can be found by ad number or phone.

What I tested
  • Sorting by nearest, price and most recent
  • Filters for city, district, transaction and property type, and the result count
  • Recent searches, saved filters and reset
  • Search by ad number or phone
02Maps & location

Map search & location accuracy

Properties for rent and for sale shown on a map of Jordan, with filters by type and region and a switch to list view.

What I tested
  • Location-based services and mapping accuracy
  • Map filters by type and region
  • Switching between map and list views
03Communication & account

In-app chat & user account

Buyers and sellers talk through in-app messages; the account area holds personal info, favourite and hidden ads, alerts, support, and app settings for notifications and language.

What I tested
  • In-app chat and communication features
  • REST API automation for chat and user interactions
  • Account flows: favourites, hidden ads, alerts and support
  • App settings: notifications and language
