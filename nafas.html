01AI features

AI-generated workout plans

Users describe a goal or attach a photo or PDF of a program, and the AI builds a workout plan; an AI coach answers questions and AI analysis reviews the journey.

What I tested
  • Generating a program from a description, photo or PDF
  • AI coach responses and suggested prompts
  • AI analysis on the program report
  • Generated programs appearing in My programs
02Workouts

Workout creation & program flows

Starting a workout from a program, creating a new workout or building one with AI, and following the program day by day.

What I tested
  • Creating and starting workouts
  • Active program and session flow
  • Program days and their exercises
  • Consistency between dashboard and program data
03Progress

Progress tracking & personal records

Program reports summarise completion, adherence and streaks; personal records track the heaviest lifts.

What I tested
  • Program report figures and completion percentage
  • Personal records and recent workouts
  • Progress updates after finishing sessions
04Nutrition

Nutrition & meal logging

Daily intake against calorie and macro targets, meal logging, and editable nutrition goals.

What I tested
  • Meal logging and daily intake totals
  • Calorie and macro calculations
  • Editing targets for cut, maintain and bulk
05Data & uploads

InBody uploads & data extraction

Users upload an InBody result as a photo or PDF; the app reads the values and asks the user to confirm them before saving.

What I tested
  • Image and PDF upload paths
  • Accuracy of the extracted values
  • User confirmation step before saving
