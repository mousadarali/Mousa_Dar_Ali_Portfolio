01Quran & Mushaf

Reading the Quran

Surah and Juz browsing, Mushaf page images, written text with Tafsir, and reading progress that is saved automatically.

What I tested
  • Navigation between Surah, Juz and Tafsir tabs
  • Mushaf page rendering and page-by-page navigation
  • Verse actions: play, copy and Tafsir, plus font size and search
  • Reading progress saved automatically
02Khatma planner

Completing the Quran on a plan

Users pick a duration, the app splits the Mushaf into a daily portion, tracks progress through the week and can remind them in the evening.

What I tested
  • Daily portion calculated for each duration (7 to 90 days)
  • Progress tracking: daily, weekly and overall
  • Daily reminder settings and push notifications
  • Restarting a Khatma and keeping data consistent
03Audio & streaming

Quran radio, recitations & live TV

Quran radio with 100+ reciters, recitation videos, background radio channels and live TV streaming.

What I tested
  • Playback stability for radio and recitations
  • Reciter search and selection
  • Background audio while using other apps
  • Live TV streaming for both channels
04Location-based features

Prayer times & Qibla direction

Prayer times by location with Hijri and Gregorian dates and a countdown to the next prayer, plus Qibla direction in simple and compass modes.

What I tested
  • Prayer times accuracy for the user's location
  • Countdown to the next prayer and date navigation
  • Qibla direction in simple and compass modes
  • Distance to Makkah and rotation guidance
05Adhkar, Hadith & Tasbih

Daily remembrance

What I tested
